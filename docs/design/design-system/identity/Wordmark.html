Bookmark

A ribbon marking your place in the material — refined away from the stock bookmark glyph. Two things make it CommitAhead's and not anyone's: the notch is squared and off-centre to the right, so the mark leans forward and the tails are deliberately unequal; and the ribbon carries two cut slots of decreasing length — the ranked queue, inside the marker. Both moves come from the product, not from decoration.

Construction — 32 × 46 unit grid

Notch. A 4-unit flat apex spanning x 18.4–22.4, right of the 16-unit centre line. Squared, never pointed — a sharp V would be the only acute angle in a system whose corners top out at 4px. The left tail is 18.4 units wide, the right 9.6: a 2:1 imbalance that reads as forward motion rather than as an error.

Slots. 3 units tall, at y 11 and y 18, widths 20 and 13. Same descending-length logic as a ranked queue, and the same 3-unit rule weight used for the active-tab underline. Inset 6 units from the left, flush-ended — they read as cuts in paper, not as a drawn stripe.

Corners. 2-unit radius on the two top corners only. The tails end square, as a cut ribbon does.

monochrome paper — knockout on any dark ground

Alignment is optical, not geometric. The mark is 42 units tall against a 34.4-unit cap height, and it is set so its own visual centre — which sits above the geometric centre, because the notch removes mass from the bottom — lands on the centre of the cap band. In practice the mark's box runs from 13.8 to 55.8 while the cap band runs 17.6 to 52: it overhangs the baseline by 3.8 and the cap line by 3.8, evenly. Centring the two boxes instead makes the mark look like it is floating.

Gap between mark and wordmark is 15.8 units at a 48-unit type size — 0.46 × cap height, and equal to the mark's right tail. Clear space on all four sides is the width of that right tail (9.6 units at mark scale, ≈ 0.21 × mark height). Nothing enters it.

Small-size legibility

Three cuts, by size. Above 22px the full mark carries both slots. From 14 to 22px, symbol-sm.svg replaces them with a single 3.5-unit slot — two slots at that scale sit less than a pixel apart and grey into a smudge. Below 14px, symbol-solid.svg drops the slots entirely and relies on the silhouette, which is where the off-centre notch earns its place: it is still identifiable at 12px, where a symmetric bookmark is not. The favicon uses the small cut on a navy 8-radius square.

In the product the mark almost never appears at size. It is 14px in the sidebar beside a 17px wordmark set live in Public Sans, and 16px in the browser tab. The full lockup is reserved for the login screen, exported CV footers, and anything printed.

The wordmark in UI chrome is live type, not the SVG — Commit at weight 700 and Ahead at 400 in --accent, tracked −3%. Only the standalone lockup files carry outlines.

Do not

Do not mirror the notchFlipping the notch to the left reverses the forward lean, which is the mark's only asymmetry and its whole point.
Do not recolour the slotsThey are cuts, not elements. They show whatever is behind the mark, and the mark itself is a single colour.
Do not set the wordmark in another facePublic Sans Bold + Regular, tracked −3.5%, split exactly at Commit/Ahead. No italic, no all-caps, no letter-spacing changes.
Do not place the mark on a photograph or gradientThere is no imagery in this brand. On any non-flat ground, use the monochrome knockout inside a flat navy or ink field.
