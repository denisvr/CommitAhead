CommitAhead · visual identity

Three directions — C was chosen

Decided: Direction C — Reading Room, carrying the ranked-queue clarity of A and a dense table scale from B. The refined identity lives in identity/ReadingRoom.html; this page is kept as the record of the exploration. All three took the brief seriously: focused, calm, technically credible, private, not gamified. They differ in where the confidence comes from — an editorial serif, an instrument-grade data grid, or a quiet page of paper. Nothing here is combined; pick one and it becomes the design system's tokens, components and UI kits.

Direction A
Commit Log

The repo's own proposal, tightened. Deep teal, serif titles, hairline rows, monospace metadata — a version-control sensibility applied structurally rather than decoratively.

TypeSource Serif 4 · system sans · JetBrains Mono
AccentDeep teal #106A64 — brand only; severity is a separate system
DensityMedium. Hairline list rows, 4–7px radii, one 1px shadow
QueueRanked rows with a score bar segmented by its three inputs
RiskThe serif is the whole differentiator; without it this is a competent but familiar list UI

Direction B
Instrument

No brand hue at all. Graphite, IBM Plex, ruled columns. Emphasis by inversion, so the entire colour budget goes to severity plus one steel blue meaning “AI, unconfirmed”.

TypeIBM Plex Sans · IBM Plex Mono
AccentNone. Ink inverted blocks; steel blue reserved for AI drafts
DensityHigh. Real data table, zebra rows, 0–2px radii, no shadows
QueueEvery ranking input is its own column; the formula sits in the footer
RiskReads as austere; needs excellent typography to avoid feeling like a bug tracker

Direction C · chosen
Reading Room

Warm paper, ink navy, one humanist sans. The page answers the product question literally: one item at reading size with its reason in prose, everything else a quiet list.

TypePublic Sans throughout · IBM Plex Mono for digits
AccentInk navy #24405C on warm paper #F6F3EC
DensityLow by default. No cards, 2–3px radii, whitespace does the separating
QueueA “Next” hero with a written justification, then rows 2–18 as plain list
RiskAiriest of the three; long CV and Markdown screens will need a dense variant
Open Direction C →
