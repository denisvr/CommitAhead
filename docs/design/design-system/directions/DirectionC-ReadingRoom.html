Visual direction C

Reading Room

The product's promise is a single sentence — what should I study next, and why? — so this direction answers it literally: one item at the top of the page, at reading size, with its justification in prose. Everything else recedes into a quiet ranked list. Warm paper ground, ink-navy accent, one humanist sans across the whole interface, no cards and almost no borders. It is the direction that treats CommitAhead as a study desk rather than a console.

Rationale

Paper, not panel. A slightly warm off-white ground (#F6F3EC) reads as a page rather than a UI. It lowers screen glare during long evening sessions and it distances the product from every graphite AI dashboard on the market.

One family, sized honestly. Public Sans handles everything from 30px titles to 11px metadata; hierarchy comes from size, weight and space, never from a second typeface or a colour. IBM Plex Mono appears only where digits must align.

Space is the component system. No card shadows, no filled chips, one hairline rule between list rows. What's left is the content: a title, a reason, a number. Density stays available — the list rows are 15px tall in the same rhythm — but the page always leads with one decision.

The compound splits by weight rather than colour: Bold “Commit”, Regular “Ahead” in ink navy — the emphasis sits on the deliberate act, the direction trails after it. A short 3px rule underneath acts as the system's only decorative element and doubles as the progress/underline motif in tabs and active nav. No pictorial mark; none exists in the source repository and one should be commissioned, not improvised.

Icons: Lucide at 1.25px stroke, round caps, 16px. Lighter than either other direction so glyphs sit at the same optical weight as Public Sans Regular. Used in navigation only — never inside content, never as bullets, never coloured.

Component character. Radii 2–3px, so corners read as trimmed paper rather than as software. Buttons are the only filled surfaces in the interface. Focus is a 2px navy underline-plus-outline. Section separation is whitespace first, one hairline second, a background tint never.
