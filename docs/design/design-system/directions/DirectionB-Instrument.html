Visual direction B

Instrument

Strip the brand hue out entirely. Graphite, one weight of rule, IBM Plex end to end — the visual language of measuring equipment and engineering documentation rather than software marketing. Emphasis comes from inversion (a black block, a filled bar) instead of colour, which frees the entire chromatic budget for meaning: red, amber, green for severity, and a single steel blue that means one thing only — “this was written by the AI and has not been confirmed.”

Rationale

No brand colour is the brand. A tool that is used privately, daily, before high-stakes interviews does not need to introduce itself in every row. Restraint reads as seniority — and it makes the one steel-blue AI marker impossible to miss.

IBM Plex, both cuts. Plex Sans and Plex Mono are metrically kin, so a mono score column and a sans title column align on the same grid without visual friction. It is credible, institutional, and not the default of any AI product.

Dark-first, table-native. The queue is a real data grid: zebra rows, ruled columns, right-aligned tabular numerals, 2px radii. It scales to 200 items without ever becoming a wall of cards.
