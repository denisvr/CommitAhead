Create a CV

Not built yet — this is the flow it will follow. Your profile stays untouched; a CV is a separate, saved projection of it.

  1. 01

    Job

    Paste the job advert or its URL. Everything downstream is derived from it.

  2. 02

    Region and language

    Portugal, Germany, the UK, the US. Region changes more than styling: page count, photo, address, date format, section order, whether a summary is expected.

  3. 03

    Strategy

    Target role and template. Which positions to include, which achievements to surface out of each, which skills to lead with.

  4. 04

    Review

    What was selected, what was left out, and why — before anything is exported.

  5. 05

    Export

    Saved under CV presentations with the job it was built for, so you can reuse or revise it later.

Never invents experience.

CommitAhead may select, reorder, shorten, rephrase and adapt for a market. It will never add a technology, employer, role, certification, number or result that is not already in your profile. Every line on a generated CV traces back to something you wrote.
