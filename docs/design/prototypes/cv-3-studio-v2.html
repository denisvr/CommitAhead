Create a CV

Not built yet — this is the flow it will follow. Your Profile stays untouched; a CV is a separate, saved projection of it.

  1. 1

    Job

    Paste the job ad or its URL. Everything downstream is derived from it.

  2. 2

    Region & language

    Portugal, Germany, UK, US… Region changes far more than styling: page count, photo, address, date format, section order, whether a summary is expected.

  3. 3

    Strategy

    Target role and template. Which positions to include, which achievements to surface out of each one, which skills to lead with.

  4. 4

    Review

    What was selected, what was left out, and why — before anything is exported.

  5. 5

    Export

    Saved under CVs with the job it was built for, so you can reuse or revise it later.

Never invents experience. CommitAhead may select, reorder, shorten, rephrase and adapt for a market. It will never add a technology, employer, role, certification, number or result that isn't already in your Profile. Every line on a generated CV traces back to something you wrote.
